# Project Overview

**Market Intelligence OS** is an independent applied AI and workflow systems prototype focused on market research visibility, dashboard orchestration, decision-support workflows, and operational intelligence concepts.

The project explores how multiple information streams can be combined into a structured research environment, including:

* Live market data
* Options-chain intelligence
* Watchlist and candidate evaluation workflows
* Portfolio visibility concepts
* AI/model output visibility
* Regime and macro-monitoring concepts
* Diagnostics and operational status tracking
* Paper-test review logs

The goal is not to create an automated trading platform. The goal is to explore how live data, AI-assisted workflows, dashboards, and structured logging can support better research coordination and decision review.

---

## Why This Project Exists

Market research workflows can involve fragmented data sources, manual review processes, disconnected dashboards, and limited visibility into model outputs or operational status.

Market Intelligence OS explores how those pieces could be brought into a more structured research environment.

The prototype focuses on:

* Improving research visibility
* Organizing candidate review workflows
* Making model and scenario outputs easier to inspect
* Separating research outputs from execution workflows
* Logging decisions and diagnostics for later review
* Creating a portfolio-ready example of applied AI and workflow systems design

---

## Project Positioning

This is best understood as an **AI-assisted market research and decision-support prototype**.

It is not positioned as:

* A live trading bot
* A financial advice tool
* A profit-prediction product
* A fully automated execution platform

The professional value of the project is in the workflow architecture, dashboard design, data-pipeline thinking, applied AI experimentation, and operational visibility concepts.
